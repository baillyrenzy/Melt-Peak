Daily Wellness Habits

Supplements work best as part of a bigger picture. These everyday habits pair well with a Melt Peak routine.

Hydrate early

Start the day with a glass of water. Staying hydrated supports energy and everyday body function.

Build balanced plates

Combine protein, fiber-rich vegetables, whole grains and healthy fats to stay satisfied between meals.

Move daily

A brisk 20–30 minute walk counts. Pick activity you will repeat.

Protect your sleep

Aim for 7–9 hours. Regular sleep supports appetite regulation and recovery.

Manage stress

Short breaks, stretching or breathing exercises help keep routines on track.

Track gently

Note habits, not just numbers. Progress is easier to see over weeks than days.

A sample day

  1. Morning

    Water first, then your Melt Peak serving as directed on the label, followed by a protein-rich breakfast.

  2. Midday

    A balanced lunch and a short walk to reset your focus.

  3. Afternoon

    Keep water nearby and choose a fiber-rich snack if you need one.

  4. Evening

    A lighter dinner, light stretching and a consistent bedtime.
